Hallucination / Error Types with Examples

Category Description Example
NUMBER Incorrect numeric statistics (scores, rebounds, points). Erroneous: “The score was 12–8.”
Correction: The correct score was 25–22.
NAME Wrong names of players, teams, or locations. Erroneous: “Assist by LeBron James.”
Correction: The assist was by Jaylen Brown.
WORD_OBJECTIVE Factually wrong but checkable descriptions (shot type, foul type, etc.). Erroneous: “Free throws brought the Rockets within range.”
Correction: The player actually made two layups.
WORD_SUBJECTIVE Unverifiable opinions or subjective phrasing. Erroneous: “The bench provided a spark.”
Correction: They scored 6 points in the quarter (whether that is a “spark” is subjective).
CONTEXT Player incorrectly implied to belong to the wrong team in the play-by-play input. Erroneous: “Christian Wood hit a buzzer-beater for the Rockets.”
Correction: Christian Wood was not on the Rockets in 2025; the correct player was Cam Whitmore.
NOT_CHECKABLE Requires outside knowledge beyond the PBP (career highs, league rankings). Erroneous: “Brown had his career-high tonight.”
Correction: This cannot be verified from play-by-play logs; requires external career data.
OTHER Any other mistake not covered above. Erroneous: Miscellaneous mistake not fitting other categories.
